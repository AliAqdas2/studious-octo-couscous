# In Person TerrariumEvent Workflow

Please use this document as a guide when preparing for, running, and debriefing any Terrarium events.

# Pre-Event

**As soon as deposit is received:**

- Meeting with sales staff member to determine event type, estimate amount, date and timing, venue and client preferences:
    - Who is the instructor assigned?
    - Is there an alcohol preference or non alcoholic beverage or mocktails
    - Custom add-ons? (Logo Aprons or included blank apron, glassware, etc)

**As we get the deposit**

- Sales alert in Slack
- Create fareharbor item
- BEO Shell & link BEO to Fareharbor
- Professional team reaches out to event staff to confirm availability
    - Individual staff have 48 hours to respond per response policy
        - If no response given within 48 hours, management will reach out to staff to confirm availability
- Did you contact the venue and reserve the loading dock? (insert supplier directory link and referred to venue)

**Three weeks prior to event:**

- Staff are confirmed -instructor, host
- Ops checks office inventory to firm needed supplies for function.
- Inventory is ordered, especially if additional supplies are needed to supplement supplies in-office
    - [Non logo’d aprons/Logo Aprons](https://a.co/d/fZXIjDY): custom ordered aprons sent to Basecamp to be printed
    - Ensure the following supplies have been purchases/ordered:

Supplies needed

.

- [Glass terrarium container](https://www.hobbylobby.com/Home-Decor-Frames/Candles-Fragrance/Candle-Holders/Floating-Glass-Bowl-Candle-Holder/p/38885?queryId=6ee04269ae8fdcbcc7bf2de2a9fc6c6f)
- 2 inch - Succulents
- [Potting soil](https://www.homedepot.com/p/Miracle-Gro-16-Qt-Cactus-Palm-and-Citrus-Potting-Soil-Mix-2-Pack-VB00010/316281339?source=shoppingads&locale=en-US&srsltid=AfmBOoq1kJEhNxVDJXShvfd0MvbSCyQL7fcGERJSNQIfabN5exFZoIjnULk) - 9.6 ounces per kit
- [Washable rocks (Creek Rocks - Change colors)](https://www.homedepot.com/p/Mosser-Lee-5-lbs-Natural-Pebbles-Soil-Cover-ML1123/318466890) - 14.4 ounces total per kit
- [sand bag (same brand as rocks- texture like beach sand)](https://www.homedepot.com/p/Mosser-Lee-5-lbs-Desert-Sand-Soil-Cover-1110/204370350) - 7.4 ounces per kit
- [Dried moss](https://www.homedepot.com/p/Mosser-Lee-250-cu-in-Spanish-Moss-Soil-Cover-ML0560-8/202745059) \- two colors (Dark green or Light Green) - .4 ounces per kit
- Mini Spray bottles
- Plastic bags
- [Charcoal Bag](https://www.homedepot.com/p/Mosser-Lee-2-25-Qt-Dry-Horticultural-Charcoal-0810/301896530) \- 2.4 ounces per kit
- **Decorative Ducks**
- Apron (logo’d apron)
- Chop Sticks or fork & Knife

**Two weeks prior to event:**

- Final participant headcount
    - Add on allergies documented
- Remaining balance paid
- Get final supplies needed

**One week prior to event:**

- All boxes shipped out
    - If there was anything wrong with the kits sent?
- BEO CHECKIN - Host and Instructor are contacted via phone to discuss questions, concerns, and additional event details
    - Aim to have this chat between 72-48 hours before the event in order to spot any gaps in the planning process, address questions, clarify instructions, and update accordingly.

# During the Event

Host of the event will manage the flow and ensure everyone is up to speed and instructor and painting is clear and visible.

Host takes a picture of the class smiling with their terrarium kits- multiple pictures

Immediately after the event and during your shift you post photos to the post event survey
