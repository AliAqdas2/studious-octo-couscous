# During the Event

Day of the event, please refer to the event-specific BEO for workflow information, room layout, inventory, and client details.

- Is the food presentation spread laid out in an aesthetically pleasing way? If the food has slid to one side of the trey, spread it out across the entire tray.
- Ensure each dish has an appropriate serving utensil: Spoons, tongs, ladels, etc.
- Ensure the salad is mixed. Note - olive oil is 3x more than the balsamic.
- Ensure the information posters for the dishes and their establishments are properly presented.

Event Lead remains in contact with client Point of Contact throughout the event, ensuring guest speakers speak and takes photos of the experience.

Event Lead has a few things to keep in mind [Company Handbook](https://docs.google.com/document/d/19OsGb5N7y_GIgUsYuSsfjTxVBx65Mn_zPH5_mH3grO0/edit)

Ops support has a few things to keep in mind [Company Handbook](https://docs.google.com/document/d/19OsGb5N7y_GIgUsYuSsfjTxVBx65Mn_zPH5_mH3grO0/edit)

Event Lead goes to the post event survey to upload photo assets. Post-event, guide will upload all assets to a digital database.
