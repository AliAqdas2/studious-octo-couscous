# In-Person Pottery Making Event Workflow

Please use this document as a guide when preparing for, running, and debriefing an In-Person Paint & Sip event.

# Pre-Event

**As soon as deposit is received: \___\___\___ Who is responsible?**

- Meeting with sales staff member to determine event location, date and timing, and client preferences
    - Alcohol or No Alcohol? Card on file? Drink Tickets? All you can Drink?
    - Additions (snacks, alternative drinks, etc)
    - Custom add-ons? (Aprons, glassware, etc)
    - Is the group in from out of town?
        - Yes - clay + bubble wrap
        - No - clay
    - Transportation - pick up/drop off: yes or no
        - Which company? Alberto or DC Nation Tours ([**Vendor Directory**](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1HHU1nfh-3a0UdJVzgWqRqUFxBpfeC3Y_GQT-2Serbv4/edit?usp=drive_link))
    - What venue are they choosing? (Are we going to them, or are we using a pre-selected venue option from our selection? Are there any restrictions at this venue?) [**Vendor Directory**](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1HHU1nfh-3a0UdJVzgWqRqUFxBpfeC3Y_GQT-2Serbv4/edit?usp=drive_link)

**As we get the deposit**

- Sales alert in Slack [Salesalert](https://mangia-dc.slack.com/archives/C03UU3WPUR1)
- Create fareharbor item [**How to Add Events to Fareharbor.mp4**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1b7r8q-jcbn4uZ09IftUSHdaLUuZ7FMnl/view?usp=drive_link)
- BEO Shell & link BEO to Fareharbor [**How to make a BEO Shell 4:10**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1b7r8q-jcbn4uZ09IftUSHdaLUuZ7FMnl/view?usp=drive_link)
    - Add Scissors to equipment list in BEO
    - Add Large Easel(s) to equipment list in BEO
- Professional team reaches out to event staff to confirm availability
    - Individual staff have 48 hours to respond per response policy
        - If no response given within 48 hours, management will reach out to staff to confirm availability
- Did you contact the venue and reserve the loading dock? (insert supplier directory link and referred to venue)

# During the Event

Day of the event, please refer to the event-specific BEO for workflow information, room layout, inventory, and client details.

Event Host has a few things to keep in mind [Company Handbook](https://docs.google.com/document/d/19OsGb5N7y_GIgUsYuSsfjTxVBx65Mn_zPH5_mH3grO0/edit)

Ops support has a few things to keep in mind [Company Handbook](https://docs.google.com/document/d/19OsGb5N7y_GIgUsYuSsfjTxVBx65Mn_zPH5_mH3grO0/edit)

Event Lead remains in contact with client Point of Contact throughout the event, ensuring guest speakers speak and photo assets are gathered. Post-event, event lead will upload all assets to a digital database.

The event host will track the client's drink consumption
