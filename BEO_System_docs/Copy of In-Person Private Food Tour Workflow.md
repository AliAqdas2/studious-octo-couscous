# In-Person Private Food Tour Event Workflow

Please use this document as a guide when preparing for, running, and debriefing an In-Person Food Tour event.

# Pre-Event

**As soon as deposit is received: \__Sales (Dave)\___\____ Who is responsible?**

- Meeting with sales staff member to determine date and timing, and client preferences
    - How many drinks? 0, 1, **2**, 3 or 4
        - Client confirms
        - They don’t know and will know two weeks before
    - Is transportation requested to pick up/return: Y or **N**
        - If yes, are they hosting a Dine Around or are they playing by ear?
        - If yes what address?
        - Which company? Alberto or DC Nation Tours ([**Vendor Directory**](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1HHU1nfh-3a0UdJVzgWqRqUFxBpfeC3Y_GQT-2Serbv4/edit?usp=drive_link))
- **As we get the deposit_\___\___\__ Who is responsible?**
- Sales alert in Slack [Salesalert](https://mangia-dc.slack.com/archives/C03UU3WPUR1)
- Create fareharbor item [**How to Add Events to Fareharbor.mp4**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1b7r8q-jcbn4uZ09IftUSHdaLUuZ7FMnl/view?usp=drive_link)
- BEO Shell link & attendee link to Fareharbor
- Professional team reaches out to event tour staff to confirm availability
    - Individual staff have 48 hours to respond per response policy
        - If no response given within 48 hours, management will reach out to staff to confirm availability

**Three weeks prior to event: \___\___\___ Who is responsible?**

- Staff (Guide and Op Support) are confirmed to work the event
- Inventory is ordered, especially if additional supplies are needed to supplement supplies in-office [Inventory Links](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1WSsg6tgUGXv3bYspElOWjQvYFJnnVLXXLQI2gd0oh8U/edit) **\___\___\___ Who is responsible?**
    - Additional supplies include: Bottled Water, Ponchos, hand sanitizer, hand warmers, activity sheets, cooler, ice, trash bag, napkins, birthday candles, lighter
- Did you contact the venue and reserve the loading dock? (insert supplier directory link and referred to venue)

**Two weeks Before the Event \___\___\___ Who is responsible?**

- 1 Are there any participants that are needing wheelchair accessibility assistance?
    - Is the client aware there are few steps in each tour?
- 2\. What are the locations that we will be visiting?
    - 2.a Did you contact the locations to place reservations and place the specific orders at each location [**Vendor Directory**](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1HHU1nfh-3a0UdJVzgWqRqUFxBpfeC3Y_GQT-2Serbv4/edit?usp=drive_link)
    - 2.b are the time windows reserved at each location (add 45 between stops)
    - 2\. c if you reserve this far in advance, did you contact them again to remind them 72 hrs in advance to confirm our reservation is in their system?
- 3\. How many people?
- 4\. How many beverages are included?
- 5\. 48 hours before - Is transportation reminded they are working with us and inform us of their driver?
- 6\. Do we need handwarmers or ponchos or activity sheets?
- If we need it, did management ship out the Tour Guide supplies during the beginning on month during the scheduling period
- 7\. Do we need transportation to coordinate? If so what are the pickup and drop off addresses and times for the pick up_\__ and tour start_\__?

**Two weeks prior to event: \___\___\___ Who is responsible?**

- Details are finalized with client, including time, date, place, and special requests
- Staff are confirmed to work the event
- Attendee list is requested with dietary restrictions
- BEO is created [**How to Generate BEOs Using Form Publisher.mp4**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1nZUitVgXFg-ZzRZXAQeJKuURoqgVGoDr/view?usp=drive_link)
    - **\___\___\___ Who is responsible?**

**One week prior to event:**

- BEO is sent to everyone working the event via email [BEOs](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1iewTAp1f-f_SPPPcvKOcjv4jy3kFmDqL?usp=drive_link) **\___\___\___ Who is responsible?**
    - Inventory is triple-checked to ensure that everything is in-office **\___\___\___ Who is responsible?**
        - Any remaining needed supplies are picked up either in-person shopping, using curbside delivery, or rush shipping items.
- Day before the event Guide and Support are contacted via phone to discuss questions, concerns, and additional event details **\___\___\___ Who is responsible?**
    - Coordinate time to have this chat between 72-48 hours before the event in order to spot any gaps in the planning process, address questions, and clarify instructions from the BEO and leadership team.
- BEO is printed and mailed to the guide responsible for the event

# During the Event

- Day of the event, please refer to the event-specific BEO for workflow information, room layout, inventory, and client details.
- Guide has a few things to keep in mind [Company Handbook](https://docs.google.com/document/d/19OsGb5N7y_GIgUsYuSsfjTxVBx65Mn_zPH5_mH3grO0/edit)
- Guide remains in contact with client Point of Contact throughout the event, ensuring guest speakers speak and takes photos of the experience.
- Guide goes to the tour guide post tour survey to upload photo assets. Post-event, guide will upload all assets to a digital database.
