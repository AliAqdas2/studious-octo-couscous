Venue Guidelines

The purpose of this document is to identify the terms of use for each individual venue we operate events through. These guidelines are meant to be best practice use of the venue spaces, and will be updated accordingly as venues communicate new expectations with our team; however, regardless of what is stated here, on-site venue coordinators such as venue staff will always have the final say on the use of their spaces.

Whittemore House

  • Whittemore House kitchen supplies (i.e. cups/forks/plates, etc.) are not to be used for events. Whittemore House is not equipped to provide these items for events unless requested in advance.
  • After the conclusion of the event, Mangia DC is allowed to use Whittemore House’s kitchen to clean supplies, but all supplies must be dried using our own towels.
  • If Mangia DC staff are unaware of how to utilize Whittemore House’s dishwasher, please ask a member of Whittemore House’s staff
  • Trash is to be removed at the conclusion of the event to the dumpsters located outside. Please recycle all recyclables in the proper location as well.
  • Mangia DC is allowed to utilize the fridge and freezer, but all food must be removed at the conclusion of the event.
  • The space is to be left how it was found at the conclusion of the event – tables wiped down so the wine does not stain their tables, no food left in the sink, chairs returned to their original locations, etc.

Mr. Smith’s of Georgetown

  • Mr. Smith’s kitchen supplies (i.e. cups/forks/plates, etc.) are not to be used for events. Mr. Smith’s is not equipped to provide these items for events unless requested in advance.
  • Mangia DC staff are not allowed to go into Mr. Smith’s kitchen or bar areas.
  • If filling pots with water, staff may use the bar sink. Other than that, no dumping, food removal, cleaning, etc is to be done in Mr. Smith’s drains to prevent clogging.
  • No pouring liquids, grease, or food down drains, toilets, or trash cans. Ask Mr. Smith’s staff to dispose of all food waste for you to ensure it ends up in the proper receptacle.
  • After the conclusion of the event, clean up is typically done by Mr. Smith’s staff, as our team is not allowed back in the kitchen. Request a plastic tub for dirty dishes and request Mr. Smith’s staff assist with cleanup.
  • Mangia DC utilizes Mr. Smith’s bar. When drinks are provided at events, request event drink receipt from bartender (request this before and after the event)
  • Trash is to be removed at the conclusion of the event to the dumpsters located outside.
  • The space is to be left how it was found at the conclusion of the event – tables wiped down so the wine does not stain their tables, no food left in the sink, chairs returned to their original locations, etc.

City Tavern

  • City Tavern kitchen supplies (i.e. cups/forks/plates, etc.) are allowed to be used for events, but permission should be gotten while on-site from a venue manager like Brian B.
  • All supplies borrowed should be returned to the designated area at the conclusion of the event.
  • City Tavern’s kitchen staff and team will wash dishes, DO NOT USE DISHWASHER.
  • Trash is to be removed at the conclusion of the event. Ask City Tavern staff to direct our team on the best location to take refuse.
  • Mangia DC is allowed to utilize the fridge and freezer, but all food must be removed at the conclusion of the event.
  • The space is to be left how it was found at the conclusion of the event – tables wiped down so the wine does not stain their tables, no food left in the sink, chairs returned to their original locations, etc.
  • This venue has multiple rental rooms. Confirm with staff which rooms we do/do not have access to, then Mangia DC staff are responsible for ensuring guests of the event are kept in approved and open spaces.

Wingos

  • Wingos allows us to use their dinnerware during events, including plates, silverware, bowls, and glasses. We are responsible for making sure we treat their supplies with respect, and must provide our own cooking supplies (pots/pans/cooking utensils) separately.
  • Trash is to be removed at the conclusion of the event to the dumpsters located outside.
  • The space is to be left how it was found at the conclusion of the event – tables wiped down so the wine does not stain their tables, no food left in the sink, chairs returned to their original locations, etc.

Launch - Navy Yard

Similar to Launch Glover Park - Pre-set Equipment in offices across from the kitchen between 4:00 and 5:00. Begin setting up the kitchen at 5:00.

  • You ( MDC notes: a manager with an event host's first time, then event host can be after) will have to be onsite for the duration of the event

The Foundry

  • The Foundry’s Cafe is a shared kitchen space that is usable by all tenants of the Foundry Offices.
  • The Foundry’s Cafe is located on the Mezzanine level (M* on the elevators) and is accessible from the Foundry’s parking garage (Entrance found on 30th St.) or through the main lobby (Entrance found on Thomas Jefferson St.) past the security desk and to the bank of elevators to the right hand side.
  • Check with the security guard to see which way they would like you to load/unload equipment. This could change based on the time of the event. Sometimes it will be through the main lobby, other times it will be through a load-in door at the mezzanine level.
  • Check with the Security Guard to see if we have use of the Cafe Conference room, which is past the booths in the cafe and all the way to the right.
  • If we do have access to the conference room we can use this room to store our things.
  • We will also have access to the conference room kitchen where we can use the fridges/freezers, sink, dishwasher and trash receptacles.
  • Do not use refrigerators in the cafe that contain cold beverages and food items. Those are for use by office tenants only. Do not take drinks or snacks from these refrigerators.
  • Trash is to be removed at the conclusion of the event. Check in with the Security Guard on duty to have a housekeeper or building engineer give final approval on the  cleanliness of the space before leaving.
  • Leave the space as clean or cleaner than you found it.
