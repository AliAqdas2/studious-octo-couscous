  • 06:30 pm Cooking Demonstration Participants are introduced to their groups for the evening (either their table mates or pre- assigned groups), and then are encouraged to gather around the chef during the cooking demo. Evaluation criteria, printed recipe cards, and a digital version of the recipe card is accessible via QR code will be provided at each table.

  • 08:15 pm Teams wrap up cooking. As teams finish preparation, dinnerware will be provided for them to taste their delicious food. Guests can mingle while they eat, as our team takes care of clean-up at each cooking station
  • 08:30 Dessert Service Dessert is served for the group to enjoy as the evening winds down. Participants may continue to mingle, relax, and celebrate their accomplishments, while our team completes the final clean-up.
  • 08:30-8:45 pm Event Ends

As guests arrive, let them mingle, grab drinks, and get checked-in. When everyone has arrived, encourage them to find their seats and get ready to begin, including putting on their aprons.

Hi Everyone – My name is Raisa and I will be your MC today. Welcome to Mangia DC’s event for the Eli Lilly 2026 Cooking Competition. We’re beyond appreciative to have you join us here.

Do you see this logo on me (gesture to logo on apron)?  That’s pronounced Mangia (Mahn-Jah) and that means “TO EAT” or “COME TOGETHER” in Italian, and that’s exactly what we’re going to be doing today!

Mangia DC’s specialty is hosting in person food and drink experiences here in Washington DC and now experiences such as paint and sips, mixology classes, and more. We also host remote experiences delivering materials directly to your door. I want to point out that a portion of every ticket sold to all of our events goes to the local charity S.O.M.E. (So Others Might Eat). Thank you for joining us in giving back so that those less fortunate can eat as well.

Today we have three goals; we want you to have fun, learn something new, and leave with a pleasantly full stomach.

Please enjoy the event tonight as you enjoy celebrating in the moment with your peers, and unwinding with some delicious beverages.

As we begin would love to introduce you to our chef, Jonathan:

Chef Johnathan has been cooking for about eight years, beginning at age 16 in a local pub in Alexandria, where he worked his way up from busboy to prep cook to lead line cook. He later went to work with FEMA, gaining experience in high-volume cooking and discovering an even greater sense of communal impact.

While exploring culinary instructional careers—driven by his love of cooking and desire to show others that it’s not as stressful as The Bear makes it seem—he came across Mangia. His experience spans small, family-owned restaurants to Michelin-rated establishments abroad in both France and Barcelona.

He graduated from the Culinary Institute of America (CIA) with two degrees: one in Culinary Arts and another in Culinary Science, before finding his way back home to Washington, DC.

In his free time, he enjoys discovering new wine bars or heading to the Bullpen before Nationals games. Growing up in DC’s restaurant industry taught him resilience and how to make every project a joyful one.

Fun fact: he competed and placed in Kikkoman’s Original Recipe Competition, held across several cooking schools.
