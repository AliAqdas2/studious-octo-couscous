# **Adrian Patino**

Adrian Patino is a lifelong drinkmaster based in Miami, who calls DC his home away from home. With over a decade years of experience, Adrian began his journey in the hospitality industry as a server and bartender in college, but what began as a side-hustle turned into a career as he learned more and became passionate about the effect a good cocktail, spirit, or wine has in enhancing human interactions and making a moment into more of an occasion. As a Somalia level 2, his go-to drink is generally a Negroni, which he thinks is classic, delicious, and simple. That being said, he has a soft spot for classic cocktails of all sorts, especially those with whisky. He has extensive alcohol and wine training, and is currently working as a consultant with Southern Glazer’s, which is the largest distributor in the country. When asked what he does for a living by family and friends, he says his answer is usually “I’m a professional alcoholic”, which he says “has the added benefit of being true”.
