# **Handbook Purpose** {#handbook-purpose}

This handbook isn’t a contract or a guarantee of employment. It’s a collection of our expectations, commitments and responsibilities. It also serves as a reference for company policies, event procedures, safety standards, and operational expectations. Please read this handbook carefully and consult it whenever you need to.  

# **Expectations** {#expectations}

As a Mangia DC staff member, it is your responsibility to:

## *Deliver excellence* {#deliver-excellence}

Ensure guests clearly understand our message and are informed. If we do not know, we find out and inform promptly.

Serve compassionately. Ensure guests and partner eateries are treated with care. Be aware that the eatery/venue staff AND guests are your customers. Serve with the utmost attention and respect.

## *Timely* {#timely}

Please be on time for your shift. We rely on each member of the team for their specific role, and being on time is a part of showing professionalism and leadership. There is much to do before each of our events begin, and being properly prepared is key.

## *Dress Code* {#dress-code}

Employees are expected to maintain a clean, neat, and professional appearance that reflects the company's standards while remaining comfortable for event work.

### Private Events {#private-events}

* Wear business professional or business casual attire.  
* Acceptable options include button-down shirts, polos, professional blouses, dress pants, khakis, dark denim jeans without holes, or knee-length skirts and dresses with straps at least 3 inches wide.  
* Wear clean, comfortable, closed-toe shoes appropriate for standing and walking.

### Food Tours {#food-tours}

* Wear a Mangia DC T-shirt with jeans (no holes) or knee-length shorts.  
* Wear clean, comfortable, closed-toe walking shoes.  
* If is cold have a sweatshirt /hoodie with the shirt on top/ wear a jacket 

### Not Permitted {#not-permitted}

* Ripped or distressed clothing  
* Clothing with offensive or inappropriate graphics  
* Flip-flops, slippers, or open-toe shoes (unless approved for a specific event)  
* Clothing that is excessively revealing or unprofessional

## *Hygiene & Appearance* {#hygiene-&-appearance}

Mangia DC expects our staff to wear put-together clothing options that reflect our brand professionalism; unstained clothing, garments free of rips and tears, tasteful piercings, and outfits that are comfortable to complete work in are the foundation of our dress code requirements. Specific events may require professional attire or company branded shirts; these dress-code requirements will be communicated to staff prior to the event.

We also respect and permit grooming styles, clothing and accessories that are dictated by religious beliefs, ethnicity or disability. 

# **Confidentiality & Data Protection** {#confidentiality-&-data-protection}

We want to ensure that private information about clients, staff members, partners and our company is well-protected.  
Examples of confidential information are:

* Staff records  
* Unpublished financial information  
* Data of customers/partners/vendors  
* Customer lists (existing and prospective)  
* Unpublished goals, forecasts and initiatives marked as confidential

You must:

* Lock or secure confidential information at all times.  
* Shred confidential documents when they're no longer needed.  
* Make sure you view confidential information on secure devices only.  
* Only disclose information to other staff when it's necessary and authorized.  
* Keep confidential documents inside our company's premises unless it's absolutely necessary to move them.

You must not:

* Use confidential information for your personal benefit or profit.  
* Disclose confidential information to anyone outside of our company.  
* Replicate confidential documents and files and store them on insecure devices.

This policy is important for our company's legality and reputation. We will terminate any staff member who breaches our confidentiality guidelines for personal profit. We may also discipline any unintentional breach of this policy depending on its frequency and seriousness. We will terminate staff who repeatedly disregard this policy, even when they do so unintentionally.

# **Recruitment & Onboarding Workflow** {#recruitment-&-onboarding-workflow}

## *Purpose* {#purpose}

This workflow standardizes the recruitment, hiring, onboarding, and training process for employees, interns, practicum students, and independent contractors.

## *Phase 1: Recruitment* {#phase-1:-recruitment}

### Step 1: Identify Hiring Need {#step-1:-identify-hiring-need}

The hiring manager determines the need for additional staff based on operational demands, seasonal events, or internship opportunities.

### Step 2: Recruit Candidates {#step-2:-recruit-candidates}

Candidates may be recruited through career fairs, university partnerships, online job boards, employee referrals, or company postings.

### Step 3: Review Applications {#step-3:-review-applications}

Applications are reviewed to determine whether the candidate should move forward, provide additional information, or be declined.

### Step 4: Schedule Interviews {#step-4:-schedule-interviews}

Belle contacts qualified candidates within **48 hours** to schedule interviews and provide interview details.

## *Phase 2: Hiring* {#phase-2:-hiring}

### Step 5: Conduct Interviews {#step-5:-conduct-interviews}

Interviews assess the candidate's experience, availability, communication skills, and overall fit for the position.

### Step 6: Make a Hiring Decision {#step-6:-make-a-hiring-decision}

Qualified candidates receive an offer, while other applicants may be waitlisted or respectfully declined.

## *Phase 3: New Hire Paperwork* {#phase-3:-new-hire-paperwork}

New hires complete all required employment documents, including tax forms, direct deposit information, company agreements, and any position-specific paperwork. Finance receives the new hire's position, pay rate, and employment classification for payroll setup.

## *Phase 4: Onboarding* {#phase-4:-onboarding}

New hires are introduced to the company, workplace expectations, communication procedures, safety guidelines, and operational standards before beginning work.

## *Phase 5: Training* {#phase-5:-training}

Employees complete hands-on training through shadow shifts, operational instruction, and customer service training until they demonstrate competency.

## *Phase 6: Career Progression* {#phase-6:-career-progression}

Experienced employees may advance to **Senior Event Support Associate**, where they assist with training new team members and supporting event operations. Continued growth may lead to future leadership opportunities.

## *Shadow Training* {#shadow-training}

All new Event Support  Associates complete a structured three-shift training program before working independently. The goal of this program is to provide hands-on experience while ensuring new team members feel confident supporting events and representing Mangia DC.

### Training Shift  2 {#training-shift-2}

* Shadow an experienced team member for the entire event.  
* Observe event setup, guest interactions, inventory management, event flow, and breakdown procedures.   
* Assist with tasks under the direction of the trainer, taking a more active role in setup and cleanup while receiving coaching throughout the event.  
* This shift is paid at the designated training rate.

### Training Shift 3 {#training-shift-3}

* Work in your assigned position at the standard position pay rate.  
* A Senior Team Member or designated trainer will remain available throughout the event to provide guidance, answer questions, and ensure you are comfortable performing your responsibilities independently.  
* Successful completion of this shift marks the completion of the initial onboarding training process.

# **Availability**  {#availability}

At Mangia DC, scheduling happens on a monthly basis. Employees receive an email requesting their availability for the upcoming month. Once availability is collected, schedules are created and distributed. Staff are expected to review their schedule and communicate any conflicts before the deadline provided.

New hires should submit their availability as soon as onboarding is complete so they can be included in the next scheduling cycle.

# **Scheduling** {#scheduling}

* Scheduling is completed on a monthly basis using employee availability, training status, qualifications, and business needs.  
* Employees will receive a monthly request to submit their availability for the upcoming schedule. Availability must be submitted by the stated deadline.  
* Schedules are typically distributed at least 10 days before the start of the new month. Employees are responsible for reviewing their assigned shifts and reporting any conflicts before the confirmation deadline.  
* Once the confirmation deadline has passed, all assigned shifts are considered accepted, and employees are expected to report on time and prepared to work.  
* Mangia DC uses FareHarbor for staff scheduling. Employees are expected to regularly review their schedule for event details, assigned roles, locations, and updates.  
* Schedule changes, including event additions, cancellations, or time adjustments, will be communicated as soon as possible via email or text. Employees should respond promptly to these updates.  
* If a scheduling conflict arises after a schedule has been confirmed, staff members must coordinate with other members before notifying management immediately. Employees may not independently swap or transfer shifts without management approval.  
* Frequent cancellations, missed shifts, or failure to submit availability may impact future scheduling opportunities and performance evaluations.

# **Invoicing & Payroll** {#invoicing-&-payroll}

* Track your hours for each event and submit your invoice at the end of every month by the required deadline.  
* Payroll is processed after monthly invoices are approved and received. Direct deposits can be expected within the first full week of the following month.  
* Pre-approved business expenses will be reimbursed when submitted with itemized receipts.  
* If a client cancels an event after you have been scheduled, you will receive a $25 cancellation fee for reserving your availability.

# **Communication & Reporting** {#communication-&-reporting}

Effective communication is essential to maintaining safe, organized, and successful event operations. Employees are expected to communicate professionally, respond promptly, and keep management informed of any issues that may affect event execution or team performance.

## *Communication Expectations* {#communication-expectations}

Employees are expected to:

* Maintain professional and respectful communication with coworkers, clients, vendors, and guests.  
* Respond to work-related messages, emails, or phone calls in a timely manner during scheduled work hours or when on call.  
* Notify their supervisor immediately if they will be late, absent, or unable to fulfill a scheduled shift.  
* Ask questions or seek clarification whenever instructions or expectations are unclear.  
* Keep managers informed of any changes, delays, or concerns that may impact an event or daily operations.

## *Reporting Procedures* {#reporting-procedures}

Employees are responsible for reporting the following to their supervisor as soon as possible:

* Safety hazards or workplace injuries.  
* Equipment damage, missing inventory, or supply shortages.  
* Client concerns or service issues.  
* Scheduling conflicts or availability changes.  
* Accidents, incidents, or emergencies occurring before, during, or after an event.

Reports should include accurate details, including the date, time, location, individuals involved, and any actions already taken.

## *Chain of Communication* {#chain-of-communication}

Employees should follow the established chain of communication when reporting questions or concerns:

1. Immediate Supervisor or Event Lead  
2. Operations Manager  
3. Senior Management (if necessary)

Questions or concerns should be addressed through the appropriate supervisor before being escalated unless the situation requires immediate management intervention.

# **Professional Standards** {#professional-standards}

   Represent Mangia DC through appropriate behavior, communication, appearance, and a positive attitude.  
* **Limit personal phone use and avoid eating in front of guests.**

* **Prioritize guest safety and satisfaction.**  
   Every team member is responsible for creating a safe, enjoyable, and memorable experience for guests.

# **Emergency Response** {#emergency-response}

At Mangia DC, guest and team safety is the highest priority. During any emergency or safety concern, team members should remain calm, follow proper procedures, and immediately communicate with the Event Lead.

* **Stay calm and prioritize safety.**  
   Remain professional, assess the situation, and focus on keeping guests and team members safe.  
* **Notify the Event Lead immediately.**  
   Report any emergency, safety concern, guest incident, or unexpected situation so the Event Lead can coordinate the appropriate response.

  ## *Medical Emergencies* {#medical-emergencies}

* **Call 911 if needed.**  
   For serious medical situations, contact emergency services immediately and provide accurate location details.  
* **Notify the Event Lead and Management.**  
   Communicate what happened, where the incident occurred, and any actions already taken.  
* **Maintain professionalism and guest privacy.**  
   Avoid discussing medical situations with guests and allow leadership to manage communication.

  ## *Fire & Evacuation* {#fire-&-evacuation}

* **Follow venue evacuation procedures.**  
   Listen to instructions from venue staff, the Event Lead, or emergency personnel.  
* **Assist guests in exiting safely.**  
   Help guide guests calmly to designated exits while maintaining a professional presence.  
* **Do not re-enter until authorized.**  
   Return to the event space only after receiving approval from appropriate personnel.

  ## *Safety Hazards & Incident Reporting* {#safety-hazards-&-incident-reporting}

* **Report hazards or damaged equipment immediately.**  
   Notify the Event Lead of spills, unsafe conditions, damaged equipment, or anything that may impact guest safety.  
* **Report guest or safety incidents promptly.**  
   Communicate any accidents, injuries, complaints, or concerns to ensure proper follow-up.  
* **Do not attempt repairs or handle situations beyond your role.**  
   Prioritize safety and allow the Event Lead or appropriate personnel to manage the response.
